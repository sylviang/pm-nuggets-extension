# Logos, Pathos, and Ethos

_Last updated: 2025-12-14_

**Logos, Pathos, and Ethos** are Aristotle's three modes of persuasion, essential for PMs influencing stakeholders, communicating vision, and driving adoption.

1. **Logos (Logic)**: Appeals to reason through data, facts, and evidence. i.e. Metrics, A/B tests, ROI calculations, market analysis, data-driven prioritization, market sizing, feature benefits, pricing transparency.
2. **Pathos (Emotion)**: Appeals to feelings and values through storytelling. i.e. User stories, vision statements, problem narratives, vision of customer impact, emotional brand connection.
3. **Ethos (Credibility)**: Establishes trust through expertise and reputation. i.e. Case studies, customer logos, team credentials, industry recognition, past launch successes, trust signals (certifications, testimonals).

Effective persuasion combines all three, balancing rational arguments with emotional resonance and credible delivery.
