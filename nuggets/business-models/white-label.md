# White Label

_Last updated: 2025-12-14_

**White label** is when one company produces a product and another rebrands and resells it as their own. The producer creates a generic version; partners apply their identity, logo, and positioning.

**How it works**:
- **Producer**: Builds and maintains core product with multi-brand infrastructure
- **Reseller**: Licenses, brands, customizes, and markets to their customers
- **End customer**: Typically unaware—sees only reseller's brand

**For white-label providers**:
- Scalable distribution through partners
- Faster growth (multiple partners selling)
- Reduced marketing costs
- Platform economics (one product, many brands)

**For white-label consumers**:
- Faster time to market (no building from scratch)
- Lower development costs
- Focus on brand and customer relationships
- Proven product reduces risk

**Product considerations**: Customization flexibility, multi-tenancy architecture, partner enablement tools, pricing complexity, feature parity decisions, quality control.

**Challenges**: Brand dilution, partner dependency, competitive cannibalization, support complexity, distance from end-user feedback.
