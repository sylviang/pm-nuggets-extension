# Rent Instead of Buy

_Last updated: 2025-12-14_

**Rent instead of buy** provides customers access through rental or subscription rather than purchase, shifting the value proposition from ownership to access. This enables flexible use without long-term commitment or capital investment. 

The rental model addresses affordability, maintenance burden, and usage uncertainty — especially for high-value items or infrequently used products. The model creates recurring revenue while building ongoing relationships and enabling usage-based pricing.

Key customer advantages:
- Lower entry barriers without large upfront costs
- Flexibility to use only when needed
- Provider handles maintenance and upgrades
- Try-before-buy without commitment
- Supports sustainability and circular economy

Common applications:
- Mobility services (bikes, scooters, cars)
- Furniture and fashion rental
- SaaS subscriptions
- Equipment and tools for projects
- Streaming content vs. purchasing
