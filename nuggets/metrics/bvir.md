# Big Visible Information Radiator (BVIR)

_Last updated: 2025-12-14_

**BVIR** is a large, prominently displayed visualization of key project information, metrics, and progress visible to the entire team. Popularized by Alistair Cockburn, it broadcasts critical information to promote transparency and alignment.

Information should be: Big (visible across room), Visible (where team works), Current (updated frequently), Simple (understood at a glance). Common examples: Scrum/Kanban boards, burndown/burnup charts, sprint goals, WIP limits, build/deployment status, OKR progress, roadmap timelines, velocity, bug/tech debt counts.

**Why it matters**:
- Transparency (everyone sees same info)
- Alignment (rally around shared goals)
- Accountability (public visibility creates healthy pressure)
- Early problem detection (issues obvious when metrics stall)
- Reduced status meetings (info always available)
- Team motivation (visible progress celebrates wins)

**Best practices**: Strategic placement, keep current, show meaningful metrics (not vanity), involve team in design, balance detail with clarity, update regularly, use visual encoding, avoid overload (3-5 metrics).
