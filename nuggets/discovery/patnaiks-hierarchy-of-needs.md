# Patnaik's Hierarchy of Needs

_Last updated: 2025-12-15_

A practical framework for understanding where breakthrough innovations emerge. Unlike Maslow's hierarchy, which focuses on universal human needs, Patnaik's model emphasizes context and activity-level needs—the sweet spots for exciting product innovation.

The framework shifts focus from abstract needs to concrete user situations. Rather than asking "What do users need?" it asks "What are users trying to do in this specific context?" This practical approach helps product teams identify high-value opportunity areas through direct observation and research. This means spending time understanding the actual circumstances and activities where users interact with your product, not just their stated preferences. Breakthrough innovations come from deeply understanding user context.

**Key applications**:
- Focus research on understanding user context and activities
- Look beyond surface-level needs to underlying situations
- Identify opportunity areas by observing users in real environments
- Guide innovation prioritization based on contextual insights
- Combine with Jobs-to-be-Done for deeper discovery
