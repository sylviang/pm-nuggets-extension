# Bus Factor

_Last updated: 2025-12-16_

Bus factor (also known as truck factor or lottery factor) measures the minimum number of team members who would need to suddenly disappear before a project is crippled. It's derived from the morbid question: "How many people would need to get hit by a bus before your project dies?"

A low bus factor signals dangerous knowledge concentration. Research on 133 popular GitHub projects found that 65% have a bus factor of 2 or less, meaning just two people leaving would severely damage the project.

**Why it matters for PMs:**
- Identifies critical knowledge silos and single points of failure
- Highlights team resilience and succession planning needs
- Affects roadmap risk and delivery predictability
- Impacts hiring priorities and team structure decisions

**How to improve bus factor:**
- Implement thorough documentation of systems, processes, and architectural decisions
- Mandate code reviews to distribute knowledge across the team
- Practice pair programming and regular role rotation
- Create mentorship programs to transfer specialized knowledge
- Use daily stand-ups to share context and progress
